02 / The approach

Less noise.
More meaning.

A considered extension of the established Cindy Santi collection: heritage-inspired color, nature-based visual storytelling, and typography that feels personal, not promotional.

Return to designs
01.

One centered image

Every composition belongs on the front. The back and sleeves remain blank.

02.

Single-color artwork

Flat vector ink avoids extra print separations. Mockup fabric shading is not part of the print.

03.

Intentional symbolism

Paths, circles, plant forms and celestial rhythms carry the message without clutter.

04.

Wearable first

No name, URL or extra branding is placed on the garment. These concepts deliberately do not recreate Cindy's canonical emblem.
